Policies & Guidelines

Reservation rules made simple

Review the key policies before submitting a reservation so your booking is approved smoothly.

Booking Requirements

  • Submit requests at least 2 weeks before your event.
  • Provide three completed copies of the form.
  • Attach a signed department request letter.

Reservation Policies

  • Reservations depend on availability and approval.
  • Events outside 7:30 AM–7:30 PM require exemptions.
  • No same-day bookings are accepted.

Facility Usage

  • Respect capacity limits and keep spaces tidy.
  • Report damages immediately to the CMO.
  • Certain venues prohibit smoking, eating, and drinking.

Penalties

  • Violations may cancel your reservation.
  • Repeated issues can suspend booking privileges.
  • Damages may lead to repair and replacement fees.
